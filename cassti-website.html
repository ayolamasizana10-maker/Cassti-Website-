Our Story

From one campus gap to six national disciplines.

2019
Founded in South Africa

Cassti began as the Campus and School Safety Training Institute (CASSTI) — established to close a critical gap in campus safety across South Africa. That same standard of practical, specialised training now anchors six divisions spanning security, marketing, women's empowerment, management, supervision and student development.

Our work reaches beyond any single campus, into the communities, institutions and organisations we serve. Every division carries the same DNA: rigorous, practical, and built for institutions facing real challenges — not theory for its own sake.

Our Vision

To be Africa's premier institute for safety, leadership and human development — advancing secure, empowered and thriving institutions across the continent, and carrying that standard onto the global stage.

2019

Founded in South Africa as the Campus and School Safety Training Institute (CASSTI), with a single focus: practical, specialised campus safety training.

Growth

Expanded into six specialist divisions and 30+ accredited training programmes, delivered across nine South African provinces.

2025

Recognised at the Association of African Universities Conference in Mauritius for leading-edge contributions to higher-education safety.

Our Mission

Four commitments that guide every division.

  • 1Deliver high-quality, customised training across every discipline we serve, aligned with international best practice
  • 2Strengthen the professional competencies of the people who keep institutions safe, well-led and moving forward
  • 3Support institutions, communities and individuals in building safer, stronger, more equitable environments
  • 4Open pathways for career growth and development across the security, education and leadership sectors

The Group

Six divisions. One register.

Each division operates to the same Cassti standard, applied to its own discipline — from campus perimeter security to student leadership development.

01 CASSTI Security

Security

The continent's first dedicated campus safety training institute.

CASSTI Security equips schools, colleges and universities with specialised, practical training and safety systems — from crime prevention and gender-based violence response to civil protest management and control room operations. Trusted by leading universities across South Africa, with 30+ accredited programmes.

PSIRA AccreditedSASSETA Accredited
02 CASSTI Marketing

Marketing

Helping institutions build reputation, trust and reach.

CASSTI Marketing partners with schools, universities and organisations to sharpen how they communicate — building brand strategy, stakeholder engagement and public communications that match the credibility of the institutions we serve.

03 CASSTI Women Matters

Women Matters

Safer campuses and workplaces where women lead.

CASSTI Women Matters advances women's empowerment and gender-based violence prevention through advocacy, training and support programmes — building institutions and communities where women are safe, respected and positioned to lead.

04 CASSTI Management Training

Management Training

Equipping leaders to lead safely and effectively.

CASSTI Management Training develops the institutional and organisational leaders responsible for safety, culture and performance — grounded in ethical leadership and real-world decision-making.

05 CASSTI Supervision

Supervision

Sharper frontline leadership, stronger accountability.

CASSTI Supervision trains the supervisors and operational leaders who keep teams, systems and safety protocols running with discipline — the critical layer between policy and practice.

06 CASSTI Student Development Training

Student Development

Building the next generation of African leaders.

CASSTI Student Development Training equips students with leadership, life-skills and personal development programmes — from campus safety cadet initiatives to career-readiness — preparing them to lead beyond the classroom.

Training & Accreditation

Accredited where it counts. Practical everywhere else.

CASSTI Security's programmes are formally accredited, giving institutions confidence in every certificate issued.

PSIRA

Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority compliance. This certification ensures CASSTI Security meets strict national standards, providing institutions with vetted, high-quality security training.

SASSETA

Safety and Security Sector Education and Training Authority accredited. Programmes are validated for quality and vocational relevance, empowering personnel with formally recognised qualifications.
